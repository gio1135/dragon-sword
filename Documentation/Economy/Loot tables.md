# Loot tables

This document outlines the macroeconomic strategy for rebalancing vanilla loot tables, injecting structure vaults, and closing infinite trading loops. The objective is to establish an interdependent triangular economy between [[Adventurer]], [[Builder]], and [[Technical player]]

## Structure vaults and jigsaw overrides
- Injected vaults: Jigsaw structure overrides inject standard vaults and ominous vaults into vanilla structures (detailed in [[Vaults mapping]])
- Persistent exploration: Vaults replace one-off chest raiding with per-player rewards, allowing all adventurers to explore structures on an established server
- Universal keys: Vaults require [[trial_key]] (standard) and [[ominous_trial_key]] (ominous)
- Regional documentation:
  - [[Overworld structures]]: Outposts, pyramids, temples, ancient cities, mansions, strongholds, monuments, and shipwrecks
  - [[Nether and end structures]]: Nether fortresses, bastions, ruined portals, and end cities
  - [[Structure loot (Modified)]]: Master index and economic flow

## Gameplay loot modifications
- Librarian rebalance: [[mending]], [[swift_sneak]], [[soul_speed]], and [[wind_burst]] are removed from librarian trades, making structure vaults the exclusive source of master enchantments (detailed in [[Gameplay loot (Modified)]])
- Fishing rebalance: Reverted to vanilla for now while planning claim block rewards for active fishing, building within claims, and managing farms
- Bartering rebalance: Maintained as the primary mass gold sink for technical players without dropping master gear

## Entity and boss modifications
- Boss encounters: The four bosses (ender dragon, wither, elder guardian, and warden) are rebalanced with repeatable, high-value rewards (detailed in [[Boss loot (Modified)]])
- Mob farming: Standard mob farms are protected from inflation while minibosses (pillager captains, evokers, brutes) supply vault keys and ominous bottles (detailed in [[Mob loot (Modified)]])

## Macroeconomic interdependence
- [[Technical player]] produces mass commodities and consumables ([[iron_ingot]], [[gold_ingot]], [[gunpowder]], potions, food) needed by adventurers and builders
- [[Adventurer]] raids structure vaults to extract non-farmable [[mending]] books, smithing templates, and rare building materials
- [[Builder]] generates claim blocks (128/hr while active in claim), which adventurers and technical players purchase to protect bases and automated farms from raids
