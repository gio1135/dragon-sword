# Boss loot (modified)

Bosses represent the peak challenge in the game. In vanilla, several bosses drop minimal or single-use rewards (such as the ender dragon dropping no loot upon subsequent respawns). The modified boss loot tables transform bosses into repeatable economic encounters

## The four bosses

### Ender dragon
- Vanilla status: Drops a dragon egg once upon initial death and 12,000 experience. Respawned dragons drop only 500 experience and zero items
- Modified drops:
  - Guaranteed [[dragon_breath]] vials
  - High chance for [[dragon_head]] on subsequent kills
  - Guaranteed drop of 1 [[elytra]] or repair core
  - Major claim block voucher (500 claim blocks)
- Economic role: Supplies late-game flight and territory expansion vouchers for all players

### Wither
- Vanilla status: Drops 1 [[nether_star]] and 50 experience
- Modified drops:
  - 1 [[nether_star]] (essential for beacon crafting by technical players and builders)
  - 1-2 [[wither_rose]]
  - 15% chance for a raw [[netherite_scrap]]
- Economic role: Powers full-beacon perimeter builds and rapid-mining zones for builders

### Elder guardian
- Vanilla status: Drops 1 [[sponge]], prismarine crystals, and prismarine shards
- Modified drops:
  - 2-3 [[sponge]]
  - Guaranteed [[tide_armor_trim_smithing_template]]
  - 1 [[heart_of_the_sea]] or [[conduit]] core
  - 50% chance for a [[trial_key]]
- Economic role: Primary renewable supply of dry sponges for ocean clearing and underwater builds

### Warden
- Vanilla status: Designed as an obstacle rather than a boss, dropping only 1 [[sculk_catalyst]]
- Modified drops:
  - 2-4 [[echo_shard]]
  - 1 [[disc_fragment_5]]
  - 25% chance for an [[ominous_trial_key]]
  - Rare drop of [[silence_armor_trim_smithing_template]]
- Economic role: High-risk combat challenge that feeds [[echo_shard]] into the [[recovery_compass]] crafting chain
