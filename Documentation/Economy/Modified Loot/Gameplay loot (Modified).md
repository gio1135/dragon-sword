# Gameplay loot (modified)

Gameplay loot systems (villager trading, fishing, and bartering) dictate the baseline value of goods across the server. In vanilla, automated villager trading allows infinite farming of [[mending]] and master enchantments, which completely destroys the incentive to explore structures. The modifications documented here protect structure raiding by closing infinite trade loops

## Villager trading rebalance

### The mending trade exploit
- In vanilla, a librarian lectern can be broken and replaced repeatedly until a level 1 librarian offers [[mending]] for 1 [[emerald]] and a [[book]]
- This exploit renders exploration, ancient cities, desert pyramids, and dungeon vaults obsolete

### Librarian trade changes
- Removal of master treasure enchantments: [[mending]], [[swift_sneak]], [[soul_speed]], and [[wind_burst]] are completely removed from librarian villager trade pools
- Rebalance of tier V enchantments: High-tier combat and mining enchants (efficiency V, protection IV, sharpness V) are removed from novice/apprentice tiers and capped at lower ranks (efficiency III, protection III)
- Role of librarians: Villagers remain valuable for selling bulk [[glass]], [[bookshelf]], [[lantern]], [[name_tag]], and mid-tier enchantments. They no longer provide endgame god gear

### Economic impact
- Adventurers gain an absolute monopoly on [[mending]] books, which only drop from structure vaults
- Technical players and builders must purchase their [[mending]] from adventurers, using farmed commodities or claim blocks as payment

## Fishing rebalance

### Current status
- Fishing loot tables are currently reverted to vanilla
- Active custom loot modifications are deferred while the economy framework is being planned

### Planned active gameplay economy
- In the planned economy rework, active player engagement such as fishing, building within claims, and managing farms will earn claim blocks rather than relying solely on passive playtime

## Bartering rebalance

### Bartering interaction
- Piglin bartering is the primary mass sink for [[gold_ingot]] produced by technical players
- The drop rates (detailed in [[Technical player]]) provide bulk construction blocks ([[gravel]], [[blackstone]], [[soul_sand]], [[netherbrick]], [[quartz]])
- Master gear and high-tier enchanted books are removed from bartering drops to ensure bastion vaults remain the sole source of netherite upgrades and [[soul_speed]] III books
