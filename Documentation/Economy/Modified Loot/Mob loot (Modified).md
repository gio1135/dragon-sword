# Mob loot (modified)

Mob loot forms the foundation of technical player automated farming. The modifications ensure that mob farms generate consistent bulk commodities without encroaching on the non-renewable templates and master enchantments preserved for structure vaults

## Minibosses and key carriers

### Pillager captain
- Vanilla status: Grants bad omen upon death or drops an ominous bottle
- Modified drops: Guaranteed [[ominous_bottle]] (tier III-V) and a 35% chance to drop a [[trial_key]]
- Economic role: Supplies adventurers with the keys and ominous triggers required to open vaults

### Evoker
- Vanilla status: Drops 1 [[totem]] and emeralds
- Modified drops: Guaranteed 1 [[totem]], 3-7 [[emerald]], and a 20% chance for a [[trial_key]]
- Economic role: Supplies adventurers and combat players with death-prevention totems

### Piglin brute
- Vanilla status: High attack damage with no unique drops
- Modified drops: 1-2 [[gilded_blackstone]], 2-4 [[gold_ingot]], and a 15% chance for a [[trial_key]]
- Economic role: Guards bastion vaults and rewards direct melee combat with rare decorative blocks

## Standard farmable mobs
- Technical players farm standard mobs at massive scale (detailed in [[Technical player]])
  - [[creeper]]: Produces [[gunpowder]] for rockets and TNT
  - [[skeleton]]: Produces [[bone]] and [[arrow]]
  - [[zombie]]: Produces [[rotten_flesh]] and rare iron equipment
  - [[drowned]]: Produces [[copper_ingot]] and [[nautilus_shell]]
  - [[witch]]: Produces [[redstone]], [[glowstone]], [[sugar]], and [[spider_eye]]
  - [[slime]]: Produces [[slime]] for sticky pistons and flying machines
  - [[iron_golem]]: Produces [[iron_ingot]] and [[poppy]]
- Protection rule: Standard mobs never drop master enchantments, templates, or vault keys, maintaining strict separation between automated industrial farming and structure exploration
