# Nether and end structures

The nether and end dimensions contain the highest risk environments in the game. In vanilla, bastions and end cities are quickly raided by the first player on an elytra, depleting netherite upgrade templates and shulker boxes for the rest of the server. The modified loot and vault integration ensures these structures remain viable for all players

## Nether structures

### Nether fortress
- Vanilla issues: Chests contain excessive flint and steel, gold nuggets, and plain iron that duplicate bartering and gold farms
- Modified chests: Focuses on [[blaze_rod]], [[nether_wart]], [[flint_and_steel]], and [[trial_key]] drops
- Vault integration: Positioned along interior corridor crossroads
- Economic connection: Sells [[nether_wart]] and [[blaze_rod]] to technical players for automated brewing and eye of ender crafting

### Bastion remnant
- Vanilla issues: The [[netherite_upgrade_smithing_template]] has low spawn rates in non-treasure bastions, leading to intense competition and depleted templates
- Modified chests: Guarantees template opportunities across all bastion types (bridge, stables, and housing) while removing farmable raw gold clutter
- Vault integration: A central ominous vault placed in the treasure room lava basin
- Economic connection: The sole bottleneck for netherite gear. Adventurers trade templates to technical players and builders in exchange for massive claim blocks and farm supplies

### Ruined portal
- Vanilla issues: Cluttered with useless gold armor pieces with negative curses
- Modified chests: Focuses on [[crying_obsidian]], [[obsidian]], [[fireball]], and [[gold_ingot]]
- Vault integration: Minor standard vault located at the portal base
- Economic connection: Fast-tracks early portal construction for adventurers and supplies decorative crying obsidian to builders

## End structures

### End city
- Vanilla issues: Non-renewable [[elytra]] and [[shulker_shell]] supplies are hoarded by early players who fly thousands of blocks in the outer end
- Modified chests: Replaces plain iron and enchanted iron gear with [[diamond]], [[emerald]], and high-grade enchanted tools
- Vault integration: Every end city ship houses an ominous vault that can reward an [[elytra]] or [[shulker_shell]] to any player with an [[ominous_trial_key]]
- Economic connection: Provides universal access to mobile storage and flight without requiring world border expansion, creating a thriving market for [[shulker_box]] sales
