# Overworld structures

Vanilla overworld chests suffer from two major economic issues: they are looted once and left empty, and their contents are filled with farmable junk (string, rotten flesh, basic iron) that devalues technical player farms. The modified overworld structures strip out farmable noise, inject [[trial_key]] chances into standard chests, and install permanent structure vaults

## Structure breakdowns

### Pillager outpost
- Vanilla issues: Low value chests containing farmable wheat, string, and bottles of enchanting
- Modified chests: Focus on [[arrow]], [[crossbow]], [[goat_horn]], and a 25% chance for [[trial_key]]
- Vault integration: Introduces a standard vault inside the watchtower and an ominous vault near the cage pen
- Economic connection: Supplies [[sentry_armor_trim_smithing_template]] to builders and [[ominous_bottle]] to adventurers

### Desert pyramid
- Vanilla issues: Once the TNT trap is cleared, the four chests contain easily farmable sand, bones, and rotten flesh
- Modified chests: Preserves [[golden_apple]] and [[emerald]], replaces trash loot with [[chiseled_sandstone]], and adds a 30% chance for [[trial_key]]
- Vault integration: A central vault located beneath the terracotta floor, protected by the TNT chamber
- Economic connection: Supplies [[dune_armor_trim_smithing_template]] and [[mending]] to all archetypes

### Jungle temple
- Vanilla issues: Tripwire dispenser traps protect chests containing basic bones and rotting flesh
- Modified chests: Retains puzzle utility with [[redstone]], [[lever]], and [[sticky_piston]], with a 35% chance for [[trial_key]]
- Vault integration: Positioned behind the hidden lever puzzle room
- Economic connection: Sells mechanical components to technical players and [[wild_armor_trim_smithing_template]] to builders

### Ancient city
- Vanilla issues: Huge risk with the warden, but chests often hold standard disc fragments, coal, and snowballs
- Modified chests: Stripped of generic junk. Focuses on [[echo_shard]], [[amethyst_shard]], [[candle]], and [[lead]]
- Vault integration: Ominous vault situated directly in the central shrine structure
- Economic connection: Adventurers extract [[echo_shard]] for [[recovery_compass]] and exclusive [[swift_sneak]] books, trading them to technical players and builders

### Woodland mansion
- Vanilla issues: Extremely rare structure thousands of blocks away that yields little beyond the totems dropped by evokers
- Modified chests: Replaces generic armor with rare materials, template duplicates, and guaranteed [[trial_key]]
- Vault integration: Secret room vaults requiring adventurers to explore hidden walls
- Economic connection: Provides [[vex_armor_trim_smithing_template]] and massive claim block vouchers

### Abandoned mineshaft
- Vanilla issues: Endless minecart chests filled with torches, bread, and beetroot seeds
- Modified chests: Focuses on [[rail]], [[powered_rail]], [[activator_rail]], [[lapis_lazuli]], and [[iron_ingot]]
- Vault integration: Mini-vaults stationed at junction rooms
- Economic connection: Technical players buy mass rails to build transportation networks and farm perimeters

### Stronghold
- Vanilla issues: Fragmented libraries and storerooms with plain paper and iron nuggets
- Modified chests: Libraries guarantee high-tier enchanted books while corridors provide navigation gear
- Vault integration: Located in the portal room
- Economic connection: Adventurers hold the keys to endgame progression, selling portal coordinates and defensive gear

### Ocean monument
- Vanilla issues: Contains only sponge rooms and gold blocks, with no native chest loot
- Modified structure: Injects an ominous aquatic vault in the central chamber
- Economic connection: Provides builders with the sole natural source of [[sponge]], [[prismarine_shard]], and [[prismarine_crystals]]

### Shipwreck and ocean ruins
- Vanilla issues: Drowned clutter and low-tier buried treasure maps
- Modified chests: High probability of [[moss_block]], [[sniffer_egg]], and cartography maps
- Vault integration: Standard vaults anchored to sunken ship sterns and ruin centers
- Economic connection: Sells botanical starters to builders and buried treasure clues to adventurers

### Trial chambers
- Vanilla status: Already uses modern vault design
- Modifications: Standardized drop rates for claim vouchers and guaranteed [[heavy_core]] rolls in ominous chambers
- Economic connection: Exclusive source of [[mace]], [[wind_charge]], and heavy combat enchantments
