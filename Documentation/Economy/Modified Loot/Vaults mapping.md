# Vaults mapping

Vaults solve the primary flaw of multiplayer exploration: vanilla chests are one-time loot that leave empty ruins for subsequent players. By injecting vaults into structures via jigsaw overrides, every player can loot each structure once per key, providing a persistent economic incentive for adventurers

## Key mechanics
- Standard vaults: Unlocked with [[trial_key]]. These drops focus on regional building accents, utility gear, and entry-level templates
- Ominous vaults: Unlocked with [[ominous_trial_key]]. These drops focus on non-farmable master enchantments ([[mending]], [[swift_sneak]]), exclusive armor trims, and claim vouchers
- Key economy: [[trial_key]] is obtained from trial spawners, structure minibosses, and structure chests. [[ominous_trial_key]] is obtained by defeating ominous trials or raid captains while under the effect of [[ominous_bottle]]

## Overworld vaults

### Pillager outpost
- Standard vault: [[trial_key]], [[crossbow]], [[arrow]], [[emerald]], [[goat_horn]], [[sentry_armor_trim_smithing_template]], [[iron_ingot]]
- Ominous vault: [[ominous_bottle]], [[ominous_trial_key]], [[enchanted_book]] (piercing IV, multishot), claim voucher (medium), [[sentry_armor_trim_smithing_template]]
- Economic role: Supplies early-game combat tools and claim vouchers to trade with builders

### Desert pyramid
- Standard vault: [[dune_armor_trim_smithing_template]], [[sand]], [[chiseled_sandstone]], [[emerald]], [[golden_apple]], [[diamond]]
- Ominous vault: [[enchanted_book]] (mending, unbreaking III), [[enchanted_golden_apple]], [[diamond]], claim voucher (large)
- Economic role: Primary early-to-mid source of [[mending]] and sandstone building sets for builders

### Jungle temple
- Standard vault: [[wild_armor_trim_smithing_template]], [[bamboo]], [[sticky_piston]], [[emerald]], [[arrow]]
- Ominous vault: [[enchanted_book]] (mending), [[diamond]], claim voucher (medium), [[wild_armor_trim_smithing_template]]
- Economic role: Redstone mechanical blocks for technical players and bamboo wood sets for builders

### Ancient city
- Standard vault: [[echo_shard]], [[disc_fragment_5]], [[candle]], [[amethyst_shard]], [[ward_armor_trim_smithing_template]], [[silence_armor_trim_smithing_template]]
- Ominous vault: [[enchanted_book]] (swift_sneak III), [[enchanted_book]] (mending), [[recovery_compass]], claim voucher (major), [[silence_armor_trim_smithing_template]]
- Economic role: The highest tier of stealth and recovery equipment. [[echo_shard]] and [[recovery_compass]] are sold to high-tier adventurers and technical players

### Woodland mansion
- Standard vault: [[totem]], [[lead]], [[vex_armor_trim_smithing_template]], [[diamond]], [[emerald]]
- Ominous vault: [[enchanted_book]] (mending, efficiency V), [[enchanted_golden_apple]], claim voucher (major), [[totem]]
- Economic role: Source of high-tier life-saving totems and massive claim vouchers

### Abandoned mineshaft
- Standard vault: [[rail]], [[powered_rail]], [[iron_ingot]], [[lapis_lazuli]], [[glow_berries]]
- Ominous vault: [[enchanted_book]] (fortune III, silk_touch), [[diamond]], claim voucher (small)
- Economic role: Infrastructure transport materials for technical players and mining enchants

### Stronghold
- Standard vault: [[eye_armor_trim_smithing_template]], [[ender_pearl]], [[iron_ingot]], [[book]]
- Ominous vault: [[enchanted_book]] (mending), [[enchanted_book]] (protection IV), claim voucher (large), [[diamond]]
- Economic role: Essential stepping stone toward the end dimension with defensive enchants

### Ocean monument
- Standard vault: [[sponge]], [[prismarine_shard]], [[prismarine_crystals]], [[tide_armor_trim_smithing_template]]
- Ominous vault: [[conduit]], [[nautilus_shell]], [[enchanted_book]] (mending), claim voucher (large)
- Economic role: The sole supply of [[sponge]] and aquatic building materials for builders

### Ocean ruins and shipwreck
- Standard vault: [[coast_armor_trim_smithing_template]], [[sniffer_egg]], [[suspicious_stew]], [[moss_block]], [[iron_ingot]]
- Ominous vault: [[heart_of_the_sea]], [[enchanted_book]] (mending), claim voucher (medium)
- Economic role: Supplies builders with rare flora like [[sniffer_egg]] and [[moss_block]]

### Trial chambers
- Standard vault: [[trial_key]], [[wind_charge]], [[breeze_rod]], [[bolt_armor_trim_smithing_template]], [[iron_ingot]], [[diamond]]
- Ominous vault: [[heavy_core]], [[flow_armor_trim_smithing_template]], [[enchanted_book]] (wind_burst, breach, density), claim voucher (large)
- Economic role: Source of the [[mace]], [[heavy_core]], and unique trial weapon enchants

## Nether vaults

### Nether fortress
- Standard vault: [[blaze_rod]], [[nether_wart]], [[rib_armor_trim_smithing_template]], [[netherrack]]
- Ominous vault: [[wither_skeleton_skull]], [[enchanted_book]] (mending), [[enchanted_book]] (fire_aspect II), claim voucher (large)
- Economic role: Supplies brewing ingredients and wither skulls needed by technical players to spawn the wither

### Bastion remnant
- Standard vault: [[snout_armor_trim_smithing_template]], [[netherite_upgrade_smithing_template]], [[gilded_blackstone]], [[crying_obsidian]], [[gold_ingot]]
- Ominous vault: [[netherite_scrap]], [[netherite_upgrade_smithing_template]], [[enchanted_book]] (soul_speed III), [[enchanted_book]] (mending), claim voucher (major)
- Economic role: The gatekeeper for all netherite gear upgrades. Essential for endgame technical players and combat adventurers

## End vaults

### End city
- Standard vault: [[spire_armor_trim_smithing_template]], [[shulker_shell]], [[diamond]], [[chorus_fruit]]
- Ominous vault: [[elytra]], [[enchanted_book]] (mending), [[enchanted_book]] (unbreaking III), claim voucher (major)
- Economic role: Mobile storage expansion via [[shulker_shell]] and aerial flight via [[elytra]]
