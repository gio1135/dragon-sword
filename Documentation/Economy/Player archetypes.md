# Player archetypes

In Dragon Sword, we have two primary player archetypes whose economies need to balance and interact with each other without relying purely on trust

## Technical players
**Playstyle**: Automate resource gathering using game mechanics
**Produces**:
- Iron, gold
- Gunpowder, string, slime, bonemeal
- Crops (wheat, carrots, potatoes, pumpkins, melons)
- Emeralds (raid farms)
**Needs**:
- Specialized building materials for their farms (glass, redstone, building blocks)
- Services from builders to make their massive technical perimeters look good

## Builders
**Playstyle**: Manual block placement, focuses on aesthetics and scale
**Produces**:
- Highly aesthetic structures, functional bases, and terraformed areas
- Does NOT naturally produce raw materials at a high rate
**Needs**:
- Massive quantities of raw building materials (wood, stone variants, quartz, terracotta, concrete, dyes)
- Currency to buy these materials from the server shop or from technical players

## The builder economy problem
Builders spend currency on blocks but don't generate currency automatically

**Potential income sources for builders**:
- Exploring and selling rare loot from vaults
- Selling manual crops or mined resources
- Selling their services: Builders can be hired by other players (e.g., technical players) to build structures in exchange for payment. This requires a trustless escrow system. See [[Builder contract system]]
